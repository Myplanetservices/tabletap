Three stops, from table to pass

Set up once in an afternoon. After that it is a QR code on the table and a screen at the pass.

  1. 01

    They scan

    The QR on the table opens your menu in the browser — the table number is already in the link, so nobody types it in.

  2. 02

    They order

    Sizes, add-ons and a note like “no chilli”, priced and totalled before they send. The order is confirmed on their phone in seconds.

  3. 03

    The pass sees it

    The ticket appears with a chime and a timer that goes amber at ten minutes, red at twenty. Your team taps items off as they go.
